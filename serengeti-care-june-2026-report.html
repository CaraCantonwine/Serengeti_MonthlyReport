What is a lead?
A lead is anyone who raised their hand — filled out a form on the website, clicked a Facebook ad and submitted their info, or came in through an exit popup. Every lead in this report landed in Go High Level (GHL), where the team can follow up. The "source" column shows which channel sent them.

Where did June leads come from?
Exit popups are the dominant lead source across the network — they capture people who visited the website (often from a Google or Meta ad) and were about to leave without reaching out.

Exit popup is your biggest lead driver — here's why that matters
141 of 192 June leads (73%) came from exit popups. This means most people are finding Serengeti Care through ads or search, visiting the website, and then being captured by the popup before they leave. This is a strong signal that ad traffic quality is high — people are interested enough to stop and leave their info. The popup is effectively the last step in the conversion journey.

Meta conversion tracking gap
Meta is reporting 32 leads via their own form tool at $18/lead — good performance. However, the Meta Pixel on the website isn't connected to GHL's form thank-you page, so Meta can't see website conversions or optimize toward them. Connecting this (via GHL's Meta Pixel integration) is the single highest-impact improvement available for Meta performance.

Branch Spotlight
Locations to watch this month
Each month we highlight a few branches that had notable performance — either strong growth, high lead volume, or areas needing attention.
🏆 Renton, WA — Network leader
Consistently the highest-volume lead location in the network. Facebook Ads is the primary driver here — the ad-to-audience match is strong in this market. GBP also shows 57 direction clicks and 28 phone calls in June, indicating strong local intent from organic search too.
📈 Lynnwood, WA — Strong momentum
Second-highest lead volume in June with a strong mix of Facebook Ads and exit popup traffic. GBP impressions of 511 in June with 59 direction clicks shows growing local visibility. One of the most well-rounded performing locations.
👀 Puyallup + Vancouver — GBP opportunity
Both locations show solid GBP activity (Puyallup: 96 direction clicks in May; Vancouver: 71) but lower lead totals relative to that visibility. This gap often means the website landing experience could be strengthened, or a GBP-to-form connection added.
