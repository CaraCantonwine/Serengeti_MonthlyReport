Yext Review Intelligence
Online reviews & ratings — July 2026
Reviews are one of the most powerful trust signals for a home care business. Families researching caregiving options read reviews carefully — a strong rating directly influences whether someone calls. This data is tracked via Yext across all active listing locations.
What is a review rating and why does it matter?
Online ratings appear on Google, Yelp, Facebook, and other directories when someone searches for home care in your area. A 4.5+ star rating builds immediate trust. More reviews also help Google rank your listing higher in local search results — so getting reviews is both a reputation win and an SEO win.

Branch Spotlight
Locations that stood out in July
Each month we highlight branches with notable performance — strong growth, high lead volume, review wins, or areas that need attention.
🏆 Renton, WA — Network lead volume leader
248 total leads, ~30 added in July. Facebook Ads is the primary driver. However, the YTD review rating of 3.40 (10 reviews) is the second-lowest in the network for an active location — a proactive review request to recent clients here would make a significant difference.
📈 Wenatchee, WA — Biggest July lead jump
+17 new leads in July — one of the largest single-month gains in the network. Exit popup is the top source. GBP Smart campaign performing well here ($1.05 CPC in June). The 4.67 July rating shows clients are happy. Worth monitoring to see if this growth holds in August.
⭐ Spokane, WA — Review star of the month
6 new reviews in July — the most of any location. Rating jumped from 3.0 to 5.0 stars, a +67% improvement. This is what a focused review outreach effort looks like in action. The YTD rating (4.07) still has room to climb — keep the momentum going here.
⚠️ University Place, WA — Ratings concern
2.67 YTD rating — the lowest in the network. Only 3 reviews, so one or two negative experiences are skewing the score heavily. Priority: identify what happened and whether a follow-up review request campaign can help offset. Also worth checking if GBP listing details are accurate.
👀 Kennewick, WA — Steady climber
+12 new leads in July, growing from 38 to 50 total. Exit popup drives this location. Combined with strong GBP direction click numbers and a perfect 5.0 YTD rating (8 reviews), Kennewick is a well-rounded performer that may be ready for increased ad investment.
👀 Franchise Search campaign
Spend dropped from $619 in June to just $45 in July with 0 conversions. This is a significant change — confirm with the Google Ads team whether this was intentional (paused/budget reduced) or an issue that needs addressing.
